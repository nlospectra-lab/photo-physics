Road to Laser Development

Historical milestones leading to modern laser science

Timeline

1950
  • Optical Pumping — A. Kastler (Nobel Prize in Physics 1966)
1953–1959
  • MASER — C. H. Townes (Nobel Prize in Physics 1964)
  • Optical Pumping & Population Inversion — N. Basov, A. Prokhorov (Nobel Prize in Physics 1964)
  • Semiconductor Optical Maser — Jun-ichi Nishizawa, Yasushi Watanabe (Patent)
  • The term "LASER" proposed by Gordon Gould (1959)
1960s
  • Ruby Laser (Cr³⁺:Al₂O₃) — Theodore H. Maiman (1960)
  • Excited-Molecule/Excimer Laser Concept — Fritz Houtermans (1960)
  • He-Ne Laser — Ali Javan, William R. Bennett Jr., Donald Herriott (1960)
  • Nd³⁺:CaWO₄ Laser — Leon F. Johnson, George Nassau (1961)
  • GaAs Semiconductor Injection Laser — Robert N. Hall et al.; independently other groups (1962)
  • Visible GaAsP Semiconductor Laser — Nick Holonyak Jr., S. F. Bevacqua (1962)
  • Nd:YAG Laser — J. E. Geusic, H. M. Marcos, L. G. Van Uitert (1964)
  • Nd-Doped Glass Fiber Laser — C. J. Koester, Elias Snitzer (1964)
  • CO₂ Laser — C. Kumar N. Patel (1964)
  • Laser-Diode-Pumped Nd:YAG Laser — M. Ross (1968)
1970
  • Room-Temperature Continuous-Wave Double-Heterostructure Semiconductor Laser — Zhores I. Alferov et al. (Alferov: Nobel Prize in Physics 2000) ; independently, Izuo Hayashi, Morton B. Panish et al.
1982
  • Ti:Sapphire Laser — P. F. Moulton → Ultrafast Femtosecond Lasers
1985
  • Chirped Pulse Amplification (CPA) Gérard Mourou, Donna Strickland (Nobel Prize in Physics 2018)
2001
  • Attosecond Pulse Generation Pierre Agostini, Ferenc Krausz, Anne L'Huillier (Nobel Prize in Physics 2023)
